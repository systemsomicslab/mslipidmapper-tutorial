Analysis overview

Choose an analysis to explore your lipidomics data.

Choose an analysis

  • PCA — Explore sample variation, loadings, and loading heatmaps.
  • OPLS-DA — Compare groups and inspect VIP plots and heatmaps.
  • Feature — Inspect class-level and molecule-level abundances and filter by chain.
  • Heatmap — Explore lipid class and molecular abundance patterns.
  • Correlation — Inspect associations between lipid abundances.
  • Volcano — Compare fold changes and significance between groups.
  • Pathway maps and export — Explore lipid changes on pathway networks.

Check your sample selection and normalization before running an analysis. Use Figure settings to configure plot appearance and shared group colors.
