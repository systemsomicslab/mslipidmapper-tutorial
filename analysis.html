Normalization and exploration

Review your sample groups and choose the dataset and display settings for each analysis.

Normalize abundance data

Open Normalization, choose a method appropriate for your experiment, and apply it. Record the method and the samples included in the analysis. You can also export the normalized data.

Explore sample and lipid distributions

  • Use PCA to examine where samples fall and how much they vary within each group.
  • Use Heatmap to inspect relative abundance patterns across lipids and samples.
  • Use Feature to inspect individual lipid abundances and their distributions across sample groups.

Check the selected dataset and analysis settings on each tab before running it.

Customize figures

Use Figure settings to adjust fonts, axis labels, and other display options. Set group colors and display order in the shared settings.
